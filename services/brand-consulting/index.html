FAQ

Questions, Answered

The things clients ask us most before starting a brand strategy engagement.

What's the difference between brand consulting and graphic design?
Graphic design makes things look right; brand consulting makes sure they say the right thing. Strategy comes first — the visuals are the expression of that strategy. We do both, and we always start with strategy.
How long does a brand strategy engagement take?
A focused positioning and messaging engagement takes 2–4 weeks. A full brand strategy with audience research, competitive audit, and messaging framework takes 4–8 weeks depending on scope.
Do you work with startups or only established brands?
Both. Early-stage brands benefit from getting positioning right the first time. Established brands benefit from sharpening what's already working and fixing what isn't.
What does the final deliverable look like?
You receive a brand strategy document covering positioning, audience personas, messaging framework, brand personality, and an actionable roadmap — plus a working session to walk your team through it.
Can you help us implement the strategy, not just create it?
Yes. We don't hand you a deck and disappear. We can guide your team through implementation, or we can execute the creative work ourselves — from messaging copy to visual identity.
