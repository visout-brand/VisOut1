FAQ

Questions, Answered

The things clients ask us most before starting a web design project.

How long does it take to build a website?
A typical business website takes 3–6 weeks from discovery to launch. Larger, multi-page sites or e-commerce builds take 6–12 weeks depending on scope.
Do you build on WordPress or custom code?
Both. We recommend the right platform based on your goals — WordPress or a CMS for content-heavy sites, and hand-coded HTML/CSS for high-performance marketing sites.
Will my website work on mobile devices?
Every site we build is mobile-first and tested across phones, tablets, and desktops. Over 70% of your visitors will be on mobile — we design for them first.
Do you handle hosting and domain setup?
Yes. We handle domain configuration, hosting setup, SSL, and deployment. Your site goes live ready to use, with no technical work on your end.
Can I update the website myself after launch?
Absolutely. We build with easy-to-edit CMS options and give you a walkthrough so you can update content, images, and pages without touching code.
