FAQ

Questions, Answered

The things clients ask us most before starting a design project.

How long does a logo or brand identity project take?
A logo project typically takes 2–3 weeks. A full brand identity system with guidelines takes 4–6 weeks depending on scope and revision rounds.
How many design concepts will I see?
Every project includes 2–3 initial concepts, followed by refinement on your chosen direction. This gives you real choice without overwhelming you.
Do I own the final design files?
Yes. Once final payment is complete, full copyright and source files are transferred to you. You own the work entirely.
What file formats do I receive?
You receive vector files (SVG, AI, EPS) and raster files (PNG, JPG) in all required sizes. Print-ready and web-ready versions are always included.
Can you work with my existing brand?
Absolutely. We can refresh, extend, or build on existing brand elements without losing the equity you've already built in the market.
