FAQ

Questions, Answered

The things clients ask us most before starting an AI video project.

How long does it take to produce an AI video?
Most short-form videos are delivered within 3–5 working days. Longer explainers or avatar-led videos typically take 7–10 working days depending on complexity and revision rounds.
Do I need to be on camera?
Not at all. We can use AI avatars, professional voiceovers, motion graphics, or a mix of all three. You never have to appear on camera unless you want to.
What platforms do you optimize for?
We deliver platform-ready files for Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, YouTube long-form, LinkedIn, and Facebook — each sized, timed, and captioned for its platform.
How much does AI video creation cost?
Pricing depends on volume and complexity. Short-form packages start affordably and scale with your content needs. Contact us for a custom quote tailored to your goals.
Do you offer revisions?
Yes. Every project includes two rounds of revisions within the original scope. Additional rounds or significant changes can be quoted separately.
