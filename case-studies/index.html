Brand Identity & Visual Strategy
Marcus · Founder, NourishFlow · Health & Wellness

From invisible to unmissable — a wellness brand that stopped bleeding ad spend.

+40% Revenue MoM
−20% Ad spend
3× Repeat purchase

A product people loved, but a brand people couldn't see. We rebuilt the visual identity around customer emotion instead of product features — and the ads stopped bleeding money.

Read Full Story

The Challenge

NourishFlow had a stellar product (3× repeat purchase rate) and a clear mission. But they were bleeding money on Meta ads — their click-through rate sat at 0.8% against an industry average of 2.5%. The product worked. The brand wasn't selling it.

The Solution

We rebuilt the visual identity around the customer's actual emotional pain — not the product's features. A validating brand strategy, attention-grabbing visuals, and a messaging shift from "look at our product" to "we understand your life."

What We Delivered

  • Full visual identity system (logo, palette, typography)
  • Reusable ad template library — 4 proven hooks
  • Messaging framework focused on emotional outcome
  • Meta ad creative for the first 60-day cycle
VisOut didn't just sell me visuals — they saved me from myself. I came begging for a quick fix. They told me my strategy was broken, and they had the guts to prove it. — Marcus, Founder, NourishFlow
Identity Repositioning & Ad Strategy
Elena · CEO, Veridian Homes · Real Estate & Construction

From price war to premium — a home builder who stopped competing on cost.

+55% Inquiries
−32% Cost per lead
0.7% → higher conversion

Strong traffic, weak conversion, and a competitor eating their lunch on price. We repositioned the brand around customer outcomes instead of craftsmanship claims — and the premium justified itself.

Read Full Story

The Challenge

Veridian was pulling 4,500 visits/month from Instagram-savvy buyers who loved their renders — but only converting at 0.7% inquiry-to-sale. Every lead cost $118 to acquire, and prospects were ghosting after the first Zoom call. Meanwhile, a cheaper competitor launched a "semi-custom" line and started winning on price. Veridian's craftsmanship was genuinely superior — but they couldn't communicate it visually without sounding arrogant.

The Solution

Complete identity repositioning. We changed the frame from product features to customer outcomes — from "look at our craftsmanship" to "imagine your life here." Ad templates were rebuilt to speak to buyer problems, not brand expertise. Customers now do the talking through proof-driven storytelling.

What We Delivered

  • Positioning audit + strategic repositioning document
  • Reframed homepage copy and tagline
  • Reusable ad template library built around buyer pain points
  • Sales-team script for "6 months of anticipation vs 18 months of agony" objection
VisOut didn't redesign my homes. They redesigned my entire go-to-market soul. They're not decorators — they're diagnosticians. — Elena, CEO, Veridian Homes
